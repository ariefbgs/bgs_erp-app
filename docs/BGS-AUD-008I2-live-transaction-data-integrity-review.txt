=== 04 REMAINING PO AMOUNT BASELINE ===
remaining_po_amount =
    po_customer_amount
    - cumulative_active_sales_invoice_amount

RULES:
- One PO Customer may have multiple Sales Invoices.
- Remaining amount is authoritative at PO Customer aggregate level.
- Invoice-level remaining_amount may only be a controlled snapshot if retained.
- New invoice validation must use current aggregate remaining amount.
- Cumulative active Sales Invoice amount must not exceed PO Customer amount.
- Cancelled/non-active invoice treatment must follow the final cancellation policy.
- Remaining PO Amount is internal system/control information.
- Remaining PO Amount MUST NOT appear on Sales Invoice customer printout.

Evidence shows stored remaining_amount is not reliable as aggregate balance,
including repeated values across multiple invoices for PO Customer IDs
59, 79, 87 and 88.

=== 10 PO CUSTOMER WITHOUT QUOTATION ===
COUNT=27

Evidence contains 27 PO Customer records with quotation_id=NULL.

This is NOT automatically classified as corrupt data because the evidence does
not establish whether Direct PO Customer entry without prior Quotation is valid.

OPEN BUSINESS DECISION:
OPTION_A=Quotation mandatory -> Quotation -> PO Customer
OPTION_B=Two valid entry paths -> Quotation -> PO Customer OR Direct PO Customer

Do not force quotation_id NOT NULL and do not clean these records until the
business rule is confirmed.
