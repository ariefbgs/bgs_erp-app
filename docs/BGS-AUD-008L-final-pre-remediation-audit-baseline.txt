=== 04 COPY PREVIOUS QUOTATION - FROZEN ===

=== 05 PO CUSTOMER / PROCUREMENT - FROZEN ===
- Direct PO Customer supported.
- PO Customer number is external customer PO number.
- PO Customer number is not forced into internal BGS numbering.
- One PO Customer may have multiple PO Suppliers.
- One PO Customer may have multiple Delivery Orders.
- One PO Customer may have multiple Sales Invoices.
- PO Supplier supports selecting specific PO Customer items.
- Partial procurement supported.
- Cumulative procurement quantity must not exceed PO Customer quantity.
- PO Supplier detail must retain upstream lineage.
- One PO Supplier may have multiple Goods Receipts for partial receipt.
- Cumulative Goods Receipt quantity must not exceed PO Supplier quantity.
- Goods Receipt detail ownership must match selected PO Supplier/detail.

=== 06 DELIVERY - FROZEN ===
- Partial delivery supported.
- Cumulative delivered quantity must not exceed PO Customer quantity.
- Delivery detail requires traceable upstream PO Customer detail lineage.
- PO Customer Delivered state is derived/synchronized from delivery coverage.
- Downstream use protects unsafe Delivery Order edit/delete.

=== 07 SALES INVOICE / REMAINING PO AMOUNT - FROZEN ===
One PO Customer may have multiple Sales Invoices.

AUTHORITATIVE FORMULA:
Remaining PO Amount = PO Customer Amount - cumulative active Sales Invoice Amount

Rules:
- cumulative active Sales Invoice Amount MUST NOT exceed PO Customer Amount
- cancelled/reversed/non-active invoice handling follows canonical cancellation semantics
- Remaining PO Amount authority is at PO Customer aggregate level
- invoice-level remaining_amount, if retained, is snapshot only
- Remaining PO Amount is internal/control information
- Remaining PO Amount MUST NOT appear on customer Sales Invoice printout

=== 08 PROFORMA / SALES INVOICE - FROZEN ===
Supported:
A. PO Customer -> Sales Invoice
B. PO Customer -> Proforma Invoice -> Sales Invoice

Rules:
- Proforma optional
- user selects the flow
- Proforma -> Sales Invoice linkage explicit and traceable
- Proforma must not consume PO amount twice
- converted Proforma not counted again as billed Sales Invoice coverage

=== 09 DELIVERY / ADDITIONAL COST - FROZEN ===

=== 10 AUTHORIZATION / ADMIN OVERRIDE - FROZEN ===
- Authentication required.
- Authorization uses role/permission/policy.
- Hard-coded email/user identity prohibited as authorization authority.
- Approval requires explicit permission.
- Normal users cannot arbitrarily select lifecycle state.
- Normal-user edit/delete follows lifecycle + downstream usage.
- Admin override requires explicit permission.
- Admin override must be auditable.
- Server-side authorization is authoritative.
- Cancel/reversal preferred over destructive delete for materially progressed transactions.
